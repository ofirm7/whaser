How Whaser uses your Google account

Connecting Google is optional. You sign in on Google's own screen, which shows exactly what you're allowing, and for each agent you choose what it may use:

Gmail

Read
Find and read the emails an agent needs to answer a customer, such as an order confirmation.
Read & write
Also send emails and replies, save drafts, and mark, archive or star messages.

Google Calendar

Read
Check your upcoming events and when you're free.
Read & write
Also book, move or cancel appointments. Google notifies the guests.

Google Drive

Read
Find and read your documents, such as a price list or a menu.
Read & write
Also create documents and spreadsheets, and update or rename files.
  • Only for the tasks you give your agents. An agent uses your Google account only while it's doing something you set it up to do, like answering a customer.
  • No copies. We don't keep copies of your emails, events or files. We store only the access keys Google gives us, so your agents can reach what you allowed.
  • Never sold, never for ads, never for AI training. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • Disconnect any time. In Whaser go to Settings → Connected accounts, or remove Whaser in your Google Account settings.

Whaser's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
